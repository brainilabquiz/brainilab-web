Before you play

What is in a general knowledge quiz?

A mix of subjects, including geography, science, history, culture and everyday facts. Choose a difficulty, answer 20 multiple-choice questions and review what you got right or missed.

Is the quiz free? Do I need to sign up?

You can play for free as a guest. If you want to keep your game progress across devices, you can create a free BrainiLab account after playing.

Can I check the answers?

Yes. You get feedback as you play and can review your answers after the round. Choose a level that feels interesting; there is no need to start with the hardest one.
