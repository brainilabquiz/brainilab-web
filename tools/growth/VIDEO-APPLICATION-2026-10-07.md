## What changes in Growth

The implementation adds an acquisition brief to the real daily Codex handoff,
not another dashboard. A task must connect an evidenced search to a reader need,
an existing page, a useful next step and a measurement plan. The conservative
route map starts with Number Route, Math Rush, Europe Flags, World Flags and
Mental Maths Foundations. Their public URLs responded 200 on 7 October.

Exact reviewed query variants can share one destination. Ambiguous phrases,
unmapped pages and unrelated/cross-page queries remain research tasks; they
cannot become verified targets merely by containing a keyword. Provider estimates
remain provider evidence. Sorting prefers matching observed candidates without
changing any recorded decision, identity or baseline. Published work is skipped
after reconciling the live state. Unknown informational articles are retained.

The existing pipeline already provides GSC/GA4 dates, author identity, original
photorealistic covers, source links, short beginner explanations, topic clusters,
sitemaps, public verification and confirmed-account tracking. These are reused.
No new paid tools, bulk pages, schema gimmicks, links or social posts are required
to apply this improvement. Daily editorial limits remain unchanged.

## Primary-source checks

- [Google: AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): ordinary SEO still applies; no special AI files or schema are required. Eligibility does not guarantee indexing or display.
- [Google: helpful, reliable content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): people-first value, clear authorship and honest expertise; do not invent experience or expand to arbitrary lengths.
- [Google: spam policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies): exclude ranking-link purchases and scaled content made primarily to manipulate rankings.
- [OpenAI: crawler roles](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots): OAI-SearchBot controls search crawling independently of GPTBot training preferences. Do not treat all AI crawlers as interchangeable.
- [Cloudflare: bot reference](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ai-crawl-control/reference/bots/): search, assistant and training crawlers are separate categories. robots.txt alone does not establish whether a security rule blocks a real bot.

log; do not repeat the earlier YouTube pilot without a different useful angle.
Candidate evergreen video: **How to tell similar European flags apart**.

> Try the Europe Flags quiz: https://brainilabgames.com/geography/europe-flags-quiz/

Candidate feedback questions: **Which pair was hardest to tell apart?** and,
optionally, **How did you find BrainiLab?** This is a proposed script, not a claim
that a video has been recorded, that these examples have been checked, or that
new distribution is authorized. Select accurate examples from the current game,
verify facts and rights, record it, then add timestamps matching the final edit.
Do not invent chapters, testimonials or user answers. No extra signup field was
added merely to implement the suggestion.

These checks support cautious application; the videos' commercial promises do
not establish that BrainiLab will rank, get cited, acquire users or sell Plus.
