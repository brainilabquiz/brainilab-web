How XP and streaks work

Your first three verified games of each kind per UTC day earn 50 XP plus 5 per correct answer, up to 300 XP per game. XP builds your level; game scores determine your ranking.

Complete a Daily game each day to extend your streak. The flame stays grey until today’s game is complete. Days reset at 00:00 UTC. If a day is missed, the current streak resets to zero; your personal best and reached milestones remain.
