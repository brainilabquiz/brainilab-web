Where is GTA VI set? Six places to know in Leonida

GTA VI takes place in Leonida, the fictional state that includes Vice City. Rockstar has published pictures of six named areas, from the city itself to Mount Kalaga National Park.

Vice City and the Leonida Keys

Start with Vice City for the urban side of the story. It’s where Rockstar places Boobie Ike’s nightclub and recording business. In the Leonida Keys, Jason works for local smugglers and Brian Heder runs a boatyard. The two places introduce different parts of Jason and Lucia’s world before their story gets going.

Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga

To see how much the setting changes outside Vice City, compare the Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park images in Rockstar’s labelled screenshot gallery. Look for Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia under their own headings. Use the place names when comparing screenshots with trailer scenes, where a location can be harder to identify.

Where to find the official map

Rockstar has scheduled a free printable map of Leonida for 19 November. The gallery is a preview of named places. The map will be the better reference for how they fit together. For the previous game, see which real places inspired GTA V’s map.

Who’s who in GTA VI? Meet the characters before launch

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are GTA VI’s central pair. In Rockstar’s opening premise, a job goes wrong and the two have to rely on each other as they get drawn into a conspiracy across Leonida.

Jason, Lucia and the Keys

Jason spent time in the Army before working for smugglers in the Keys. Lucia has just left Leonida Penitentiary and wants the better life her family hoped for back in Liberty City. Brian Heder lets Jason live rent-free in exchange for work. Cal Hampton, Jason’s friend and another of Brian’s associates, likes listening in on Coast Guard radio.

Who runs the music label?

Boobie Ike and Dre’Quan Priest are partners at Only Raw Records. Dre’Quan has signed Real Dimez, the rap duo made up of Bae-Luxe and Roxy. The name Raul Bautista comes from a different corner of the story. He’s a veteran bank robber looking for people willing to take bigger risks.

What does GTA VI Ultimate Edition add?

Standard Edition includes the full game. Ultimate Edition includes the same game and adds items you unlock as you progress through Jason and Lucia’s story. Rockstar’s list includes the ’95 Grotti Cheetah, the Shitzu Squalo boat and extra clothing. New bonuses become available across the story chapters.

How the boxed edition works

Rockstar’s boxed Standard Edition contains a download code. If you are importing a PS5 copy, check PlayStation’s region rules for vouchers before buying. Once you redeem the code, you can buy the separate Ultimate upgrade through your console’s store.

What comes with the Vintage Vice City Pack?

This launch offer includes a classic Vapid Stanier with a garage, outfits, hairstyles and a weapon design. PlayStation says the pack is included with digital purchases made before the end of 19 November local time. Digital pre-orders can also include a month of GTA+. Check your platform’s redemption and renewal terms.

GTA VI languages, controls and graphics on PS5

PlayStation lists English voice audio and 13 text language options for GTA VI on PS5. The text options include French, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, both Spanish variants (Spain and Mexico), and Simplified and Traditional Chinese.

DualSense controls and audio

Sony says you can adjust DualSense vibration and adaptive trigger effects in the game’s accessibility settings. The PS5 version also uses the controller speaker and supports Tempest 3D Audio for positioning sounds around the player.

What changes on PS5 Pro?

Rockstar describes a 30 frames per second target for the console versions. It lists sharper upscaling through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution and ray-traced shadows for PS5 Pro. If you plan to play on a second screen, see our Remote Play setup guide.

What if GTA VI won’t start on PS5?

A completed pre-load unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November. If the game is available in your region and you still can’t open it, check the download and the account that owns the game.

Check that you’ve installed the required PS5 system software update.

If the download has stalled

Open Downloads/Uploads from the control centre. Pause the download and resume it. If it still won’t move, check PlayStation’s service status and your free space under Settings → Storage. If both look fine, cancel the stalled download and start it again from your Game Library.

If the download is complete

Use the account that bought the game or redeemed the boxed copy’s code. If someone else on the same PS5 owns it, ask them to enable Console Sharing and Offline Play. If the game still shows a padlock after launch, Sony suggests using Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Restore Licences.

Can you play GTA VI with Remote Play?

Yes. Rockstar confirms Remote Play on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Your console runs the installed game and streams it to a compatible device over the network.

On PlayStation Portal

On your PS5, turn on Enable Remote Play under Settings → System → Remote Play. Sign in on the Portal with the same account. To connect while the PS5 is in rest mode, enable internet access and network wake-up in its rest mode settings.

On Xbox, check where the game runs

For Remote Play, install GTA VI on your Series X or Series S and turn on the console’s remote features. If you only have an Xbox One, use Xbox Cloud Gaming for GTA VI. Microsoft’s servers run the game in that case, with separate launch times and access requirements.

PlayStation Portal setup · Xbox Remote Play requirements

Can you play GTA VI on Xbox One?

Yes. Xbox says GTA VI will stream to Xbox One from 06:00 UTC on 19 November 2026 through Xbox Cloud Gaming. Microsoft’s servers run the game, stream the picture and receive your controller inputs. On Xbox Series X and Series S, you can also install GTA VI and run it on the console.

Check your cloud access and game licence

Xbox lists GTA VI as a separate purchase. Eligible Game Pass plans include cloud playtime, and Xbox also says players in supported markets can buy cloud time without a subscription. Check what your account, device and country support before buying.

Which launch time should you use?

Rockstar lists a local-midnight launch for the installed console version. Xbox gives the cloud stream its own start time. On 19 November, 06:00 UTC is 6 a.m. in the UK and 7 a.m. in mainland Spain. The cloud stream uses Series S graphics settings and depends on a stable connection.

The GTA VI soundtrack, score and radio

Rockstar has announced two albums, plus the music and podcasts you can hear while exploring Leonida. See the latest soundtrack album news and release listings. The separate Justice score album is due on 11 December 2026.

The soundtrack has an artist line-up of its own

Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album brings together 34 original tracks, with singles from artists including Travis Scott and Cardi B. One track links the releases. Justice’s “Flower in a Hurricane”, heard in the launch trailer, appears on both albums.

The score responds to play. The radio is yours to tune.

Justice composed music that changes with what you do in the game. The score album presents 30 tracks for listening outside it. In Leonida, you can tune into pop on Flash FM or modern Latin music on Cocoteo FM. Rockstar’s radio preview also shows how Jason and Lucia can keep listening to stations and podcasts through their in-game headphones when they get out of a vehicle.
