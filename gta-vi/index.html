Latest GTA VI news

Confirmed launch updates, from console access to the music and events of Vice City.

  1. 13 Nov 2026

    Xbox One players can stream GTA VI on launch day

    Xbox’s launch-week guide confirms that streaming starts on 19 November at 1 a.m. Eastern time (06:00 UTC). Xbox One uses Xbox Cloud Gaming to run the game. Series X and Series S owners can choose between streaming and installing it on their console. The console release and cloud launch have separate schedules.

    Microsoft says the cloud stream runs with Series S graphics settings. You still need to buy GTA VI, and cloud access varies by market and device.

    Xbox’s launch-week schedule · Xbox’s cloud gaming details

  2. 10 Nov 2026

    Pre-loading is open

    Rockstar opened pre-loading for digital pre-orders. Players with a code-in-box copy can pre-load once they receive and redeem the code. Rockstar also confirmed a 30 fps target and detailed PlayStation 5 Pro enhancements.

    Rockstar’s pre-load announcement ↗
  3. 10 Nov 2026

    GTA VI sets a PlayStation Store pre-order record

    Sony called GTA VI the most pre-ordered game in the history of PlayStation Store. Its announcement gave no total. It also confirmed adjustable DualSense haptics and adaptive triggers for the PS5 version.

    PlayStation’s launch update ↗
  4. 9 Nov 2026

    Miami gets a launch festival

    Rockstar and PlayStation announced a live music event for 21 November at Mana Wynwood in Miami. The line-up includes acts from the soundtrack, with a DJ set from original-score composers Justice.

    Rockstar’s festival announcement ↗
  5. 9 Nov 2026

    Take-Two reiterates the console launch date

    Take-Two’s quarterly release lists 19 November for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The PC release date remains unannounced in the current launch materials.

    Take-Two’s quarterly release ↗
  6. 5 Nov 2026

    New screenshots from Leonida

    Rockstar published a launch gallery with more views of Vice City and the surrounding state of Leonida.

    Rockstar’s launch gallery announcement ↗
  7. 29 Oct 2026

    Justice’s original score is due in December

    Rockstar announced a 30-track original score album for 11 December. The French duo’s music also appears in the October launch trailer.

    Rockstar’s score announcement ↗
  8. 15 Oct 2026

    The launch trailer follows Jason and Lucia

    The trailer revisits the pair at the centre of the story and features “Flower in a Hurricane” by Justice with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard.

    Watch the official launch trailer ↗
  9. 8 Oct 2026

    Leonida’s radio follows you out of the car

    Rockstar previewed six radio stations and introduced on-demand podcasts. Jason and Lucia can listen in their vehicles and continue on foot using headphones or earbuds connected to their in-game phones.

    Rockstar’s radio and podcast preview ↗
  10. 2 Oct 2026

    Vice City takes over a Miami Heat home game

    Miami host the Milwaukee Bucks at Kaseya Center on 18 November. The Heat will take the court in a Vice City uniform for the tie-in, with a one-night court design and themed concessions. Tip-off is scheduled for 7 p.m. Eastern time.

    The Heat’s game-night guide ↗
  11. 17 Sep 2026

    The 34-track soundtrack is due on release day

    Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is scheduled for 19 November alongside the game. Rockstar made the soundtrack with Atlantic Records and announced vinyl, CD and streaming editions. Justice’s original score has its own album release on 11 December.

    Rockstar’s soundtrack announcement ↗
