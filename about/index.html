About BrainiLab

BrainiLab creates quiz content and knowledge challenges across the web, YouTube and Instagram.

Watch on YouTubeFollow on Instagram

Small challenges, different ways to think

BrainiLab brings together quick knowledge quizzes, word puzzles and number games. You can play a short Daily challenge or choose an Anytime game to focus on a topic. Some games test recall; others ask you to compare facts, recognise a pattern or explain what a group of clues has in common.

What you can play

Brain Mix combines ten questions from different topics. BrainiWord uses letter feedback to help you find a five-letter word. Connections and Odd One Out explore relationships between ideas. Number Route, Sequence and Math Rush offer different kinds of arithmetic and pattern practice. Subject quizzes cover general knowledge, geography, science, history and sports.

Play first, save progress when you want

A registered account is not required to start playing. Guest games can appear in public rankings under a generated alias. Sign in if you want to keep progress across devices. The Daily hub distinguishes today’s four scored challenges from practice and past games, so you can choose whether to compete or experiment.

Learn from the answer, not just the score

The guides on game pages explain the mechanics with separate worked examples. Quizzes show feedback after an answer and a review when the run is complete. Use that review to find something to practise next. A short explanation is a starting point for learning, especially when a topic has exceptions or changes over time.

Questions and corrections

A useful question needs clear wording, a defensible answer and an explanation that fits. If you spot an ambiguous puzzle, outdated fact, missing accepted word or technical problem, send a correction. Include the game, the exact question or clue set, what you think is wrong and a reliable source where possible. Do not include passwords or payment details.
