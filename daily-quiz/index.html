A quick guide to the Daily

What is the Daily?

A shared selection of brain games that changes each day. Follow the live cards above to see which games count towards today’s Daily Brain Score.

Can I practise first?

Use Try first when it is available. Practice rounds do not change your Daily score, streak, XP or rankings.
