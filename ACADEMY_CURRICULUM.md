# BrainiLab Academy: curriculum and interactive teaching

Editorial plan updated 30 September 2026. This is a backlog, not a claim that all these courses exist. All text and activities should be original, fact-checked and beginner-friendly. Every lesson follows: concrete example, one new idea, an activity, worked explanation, short ABCD round. Each term is defined before use.

## Mathematics: from first sums to college topics

Working series title: **Maths, One Step at a Time**. Build linked courses by level, rather than a single overwhelming list. A person may revisit any prerequisite. The current three-lesson mental maths course is a useful practice branch; it is not a substitute for teaching addition, subtraction, multiplication and division from zero.

| Level | Ordered chapters | Interactive teaching idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Numbers and first sums | Counting objects; zero; tens and units; addition; addition across ten; subtraction; subtraction across ten; checking an answer | Counters to add/remove, a number line with small jumps, exchange ten counters for a ten block |
| 2. Equal groups | Repeated addition; meaning of multiplication; tables; swapping factors; division as sharing; division as groups; remainders; mixed problems | Table with highlighted row/column and product; build rectangles; share counters equally between baskets |
| 3. Fractions | Equal parts; numerator/denominator; number-line fractions; equivalent fractions; comparison; adding equal denominators; adding different denominators; multiplication/division of fractions | Split a strip into equal parts, overlay equivalent strips, fit fractions onto a number line |
| 4. Decimals and proportions | Decimal place value; fractions to decimals; percentages; ratios; unit rates; scale; percentage change; everyday problems | 100-square grid, recipe scaling, price-per-unit comparison, before/after percentage bars |
| 5. Signed numbers and powers | Negative numbers; signed arithmetic; order of operations; powers; roots; scientific notation | Temperature slider, selectable operation tree, zoomable powers-of-ten scale |
| 6. First algebra | Unknowns; expressions; substitution; balancing equations; one-step equations; multi-step equations; inequalities; word problems | Balance with removable blocks, input/output machine, shade the solution on a number line |
| 7. Geometry and measurement | Units; perimeter; area; triangles; angles; circles; volume; Pythagoras | Resize a rectangle and compare area/perimeter; rotate rays; fill a volume with cubes |
| 8. Graphs and functions | Coordinates; plotting; relations/functions; slope; straight lines; simultaneous equations; transformations | Drag a point, change slope/intercept with sliders, watch two lines meet |
| 9. Algebra and trigonometry | Quadratics; factoring; roots; polynomials; rational expressions; right-triangle trig; unit circle; trig graphs | Match roots to a parabola, resize a right triangle, link a rotating radius to a sine graph |
| 10. Precalculus | Domain/range; composition; inverse functions; exponentials; logarithms; sequences; limits intuition | Function machines, growth sliders, inverse reflection, zoom near a curve point |
| 11. Calculus foundations | Limits; continuity; derivative as change; derivative rules; optimisation; antiderivatives; area/integrals; fundamental theorem | Secant approaching tangent, moving slope marker, rectangles approximating an area |
| 12. College extensions | Vectors; matrices; linear systems; probability; distributions; statistical inference; multivariable functions | Transform a vector grid, visualise matrix operations, sample a distribution, explore a surface slice |

Each row is a proposed course, not one article. Keep chapters small. Explain the visual first; introduce notation only after readers understand what it represents. Do not advertise a full college curriculum until the lessons have been authored, reviewed and published. A completed-lesson percentage is not a claim of mathematical mastery or accreditation.

Reference starting points: [OpenStax Prealgebra](https://openstax.org/books/prealgebra-2e/pages/1-introduction), [OpenStax mathematics catalogue](https://openstax.org/subjects/math), [Calculus Volume 1](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-1/pages/1-introduction). These are fact-checking/reference links, not material to copy or repackage. Check the licence before any reuse; create original diagrams, exercises, examples and text.

## First beginner course — 30 September 2026

`maths-from-zero` supplies the first three introductory chapters: addition with two piles, subtraction with removable counters, and multiplication with equal groups and an accessible times table. Each has an original photographic cover, beginner copy and a three-question round. This is a complete three-lesson starter course, not the whole college curriculum. Division and later levels remain planned. The separate `arithmetic-from-zero-1` editorial block has 3 of 10 supporting pieces; its additional HUB remains unpublished.
