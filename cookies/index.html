Cookies & privacy choices

Browser storage and optional advertising controls used by BrainiLab.

Game and account storage

BrainiLab uses local browser storage to remember guest progress, game history, preferences and session state. Supabase provides authentication storage when a session is created, including a guest session. This storage supports the games and account features rather than marketing.

Optional Meta marketing

Meta Pixel loads only after you choose Accept optional cookies. It measures page visits and completed scored games, and may use the _fbp and _fbc cookies and standard browser, device and page information. BrainiLab sends the game identifier and whether a game was Daily or Anytime; it does not send your email, account ID, answers or score as event parameters. Practice games are excluded.

Your marketing choice is stored as brainilab_marketing_consent_v1 for up to 180 days. You can change it using the button below. Rejecting marketing stops future Meta events and removes the first-party Meta cookies that BrainiLab can access. It does not delete data already received by Meta.

Google analytics and advertising

The site includes Google Analytics with consent defaults set to denied for analytics storage, advertising storage, advertising user data and personalisation. Google’s consent mode may send cookieless measurement requests while storage consent is denied. The Meta preference above does not grant Google consent.

AdSense ownership is verified using a page meta tag and ads.txt. Verification itself does not load the advertising script. Advertising placements are gated by site configuration and consent eligibility; no ad slots are configured in this release. Google advertising consent is separate from the Meta choice.

How long browser data stays

Game history and preferences stay in this browser until you clear them or the application updates them; the marketing choice has the expiry described above. Clearing site storage can remove guest progress and sign you out. It does not delete records already stored on the server or close an account.

More information

See the Privacy page for account, rankings and contact information. You can also read Google’s privacy policy and Meta’s privacy policy for information about those providers.
