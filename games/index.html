Choose a game by the skill you want to practise

For knowledge recall, choose a subject quiz. For relationships between ideas, try Connections or Odd One Out. Sequence and Number Route focus on numerical reasoning, while BrainiWord uses letter feedback to narrow a word search.

Learn from a round before moving on

A score tells you how a run went; feedback tells you what to practise. Read why an answer fits, and revisit questions you guessed correctly as well as mistakes. Each game has its own guide with a teaching example separate from the live challenge.
