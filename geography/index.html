Before you play

Which geography quiz should I try first?

Start with Europe Flags if you want one region and a fixed set of 20 flags. Choose World Flags or World Capitals on Easy for familiar places from around the world.

Do I need an account?

No. You can play as a guest. A free account lets you keep your game progress across devices and return to your Academy lessons. The Europe practice round stays on its results page and does not award XP or ranking points.

Can I review my mistakes?

Yes. You can review your answers after a round. Europe Flags also gives you a short visual clue after each answer, with the flags you missed collected at the end.
